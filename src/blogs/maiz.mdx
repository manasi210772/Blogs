## <u>Introduction</u>
<br></br>
Today, I decided to try Mexican food from **Maiz** and ordered it home. I ordered three dishes:

• Chipotle Chicken Little Burrito Bowl                                                             
• Chipotle Chicken Soft Shell Tacos                                                   
• Chipotle Chicken Cali Quesadilla

Even before opening the food, the experience started on a positive note. Everything arrived in cute containers along with a thoughtful handwritten note, which added a nice personal touch to the order.

## <u>Chipotle Chicken Little Burrito Bowl</u>

I started with the **Chipotle Chicken Little Burrito Bowl**, and the very first bite instantly took me back eight years.

The taste reminded me of the first Chipotle bowl I had in the United States. What surprised me was how close it felt to that original experience.

The bowl had:

• Rice at the bottom                                    
• Chicken                                                          
• Beans                                                     
• Corn                                                     
• Tomato salsa                                                  
• Other vegetables on top

Despite being called a **Little Burrito Bowl**, the portion size was quite generous. It was filling enough to be a complete meal on its own.

### ⭐ Rating: 9/10

This is the dish I would most strongly recommend trying.

<br></br>

## <u>Chipotle Chicken Cali Quesadilla</u>

Next came the **Chipotle Chicken Cali Quesadilla**.

One thing I immediately appreciated was how easy it was to eat. The filling stayed inside, and nothing kept falling out while eating.

The flavors worked really well together, and dipping it in the tomato salsa made it even better.

The only downside was the amount of cheese. There was quite a lot of it, and at times the cheese became the dominant flavor, overpowering some of the other ingredients.

That said, if you're someone who loves cheese, you'll probably enjoy this dish even more than I did.

### ⭐ Rating: 8/10

<br></br>

## <u>Chipotle Chicken Soft Shell Tacos</u>

Finally, I tried the **Chipotle Chicken Soft Shell Tacos**.

Before talking about the taste, I have to mention the eating experience. Since the tacos are open at both ends, the fillings kept falling out from the sides, making them a little messy to eat.

Inside the taco, the chicken and salsa carried most of the flavor, but they were concentrated toward the center and bottom. As a result, many bites consisted mainly of taco shell and lettuce.

For most of the taco, the only flavor I could consistently identify was tomato.

One thing I did appreciate was that Maiz lets you choose between:

• Whole wheat tacos                                                      
• All-purpose flour tacos

So you can pick whichever suits your preference.

### ⭐ Rating: 6/10

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

Among the three dishes, the **Chipotle Chicken Little Burrito Bowl** was the clear winner for me. Not only was it filling and flavorful, but it also brought back memories of a meal I had years ago.

The quesadilla was enjoyable and easy to eat, while the tacos were the weakest of the three mainly because of the eating experience and uneven distribution of flavors.
