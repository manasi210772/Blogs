## <u>Introduction</u>


On **10th July 2026**, I visited **Chimichurri – The Asian Kitchen** to explore some Asian cuisine. Since it was my first time there, I decided to try a few dishes that I had never eaten before.

We ordered:

• Chimichurri Chicken Dimsum (6 pieces)                                   
• Chicken Chilli Garlic Ramen                                        
• Veg Chilli Garlic Ramen                                                  

As it turned out, this meal introduced me to **dimsum, ramen, and even tofu** for the very first time.

<br></br>

## <u>The Ambience</u>

Even though it was a **Friday evening**, we were fortunate enough to get a table for three without having to wait, despite not having a reservation.

The restaurant was completely packed, which wasn't surprising considering the lively atmosphere.

The interiors followed a beautiful **Japanese and Chinese-inspired theme**, making the place feel warm, aesthetic, and perfect for Asian dining.

## 🥟 <u>Chimichurri Chicken Dimsum</u>

The first dish to arrive was the **Chimichurri Chicken Dimsum**.

This was my **first time trying dimsum**, so I wasn't quite sure what to expect.

Inside, the dimsum was filled with **minced chicken** mixed with herbs and sauces. It was served piping hot, and the moment I took my first bite, it almost **melted in my mouth**.

It was accompanied by a couple of spicy dipping sauces, which balanced the flavors really well and made every bite even more enjoyable.

### ⭐ Rating: 8/10

## 🍜 <u>Chicken Chilli Garlic Ramen</u>

Next came the **Chicken Chilli Garlic Ramen**, another first for me.

The portion size was huge, so we requested the staff to divide it among us.

The bowl contained:

• Rice noodles                                                                
• Soup                                                                   
• Fried chicken                                         
• Mushroom                                                          
• Tofu                                                
• Half a boiled egg                                             
- Broccoli                                                       
* Baby corn                                              
* Bean sprouts

The very first thing I tried was the noodles.

They were incredibly **thin and delicate**, almost disappearing in my mouth as soon as I ate them.

The soup was much lighter than I had expected. It wasn't thick at all—instead, it was quite watery, but surprisingly well-balanced.

It wasn't spicy, but I felt it had a generous amount of **vinegar or lemon**, giving it a noticeable sourness. Personally, I thought it was slightly more sour than I would have preferred, but it still tasted really good.

The fried chicken paired nicely with the broth.

One thing I particularly enjoyed was the **boiled egg**. Since it sat in the soup for a while before I ate it, the broth had infused beautifully into the egg, making it even more flavorful.

Another pleasant surprise was the **tofu**. It was my first time trying it, and I honestly liked it much more than paneer.

### <u>Veg Chilli Garlic Ramen</u>

I also got to try the **vegetarian version**.

The only noticeable difference was that it didn't include the chicken or the boiled egg. Everything else—the noodles, vegetables, tofu, and broth—remained the same.

### ⭐ Rating: 8.5/10

It could have easily been a **10/10**, but for my taste, the vinegar/lemon flavor was just a little too strong.

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

My visit to Chimichurri turned out to be full of firsts.

I experienced **dimsum, ramen, and tofu** for the very first time, and each one left a lasting impression.

The dimsum was soft, flavorful, and comforting, while the ramen introduced me to a completely different style of soup than I was used to.

I'm definitely looking forward to visiting Chimichurri again and exploring more dishes from their menu.
