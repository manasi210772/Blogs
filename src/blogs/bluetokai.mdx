## <u>Introduction</u>

I recently visited **Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters** for the first time. Since it was my first visit, I was curious not only about the food and beverages but also about the overall cafe experience.

<br></br>

## <u>The Ambience</u>

The first thing I noticed was that the cafe was quite **compact and busy**. Most people seemed to be there for work-related activities—attending online meetings, working on laptops, or writing notes.

Because of this, the cafe felt quite loud. Conversations from nearby tables were easily audible, and it wasn't the kind of place where I could enjoy complete peace and quiet.

If you're looking for a productive work environment with plenty of people around, you might enjoy it. Personally, I found it noisier than expected.

<br></br>

## <u>What I Ordered</u>

For my visit, I ordered:

• **Jalapeño & Cheese Korean Bun**                                              
• **Hot Mocha**

Since I had a sore throat from a cold, I specifically wanted something warm to drink.

<br></br>

## ☕ <u>Hot Mocha</u>

This was my first time trying a **Hot Mocha**, which is essentially a combination of coffee and hot chocolate.

The drink was warm, comforting, and exactly what I needed that day. It felt hearty and soothing, especially because of my sore throat.

I even added some brown sugar, but the drink still had a slight bitterness to it. Thankfully, it wasn't unpleasant—in fact, the bitterness complemented the drink well and made it enjoyable.

### ⭐ Rating: 9/10

I would definitely order this again.

<br></br>

## 🥯 <u>Jalapeño & Cheese Korean Bun</u>

The **Jalapeño & Cheese Korean Bun** had a very interesting flavor profile.

The bun itself was soft and easy to chew. Inside, the filling tasted somewhat like garlic butter and contained cheese, jalapeños, and other ingredients that worked really well together.

The combination of flavors was unique and enjoyable.

The only downside was that the filling didn't extend all the way to the bottom of the bun. As I got closer to the end, most bites consisted mainly of baked dough with very little filling remaining.

Even with that minor issue, I still enjoyed it and felt it was worth trying.

### ⭐ Rating: 8/10

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

My first visit to Blue Tokai was a pleasant one.

The cafe itself was louder and busier than I expected, but the food and drinks made up for it. The Hot Mocha was comforting and satisfying, while the Jalapeño & Cheese Korean Bun offered a unique combination of flavors that stood out.

If I were to visit again, the Hot Mocha would definitely be on my order list.
