## <u>Introduction</u>

After getting influenced by yet another social media reel, I decided to try the **Molten Cookie with Ice Cream** at **Kill No Kalorie Cafe**, located on Ghantali Road, Thane.

The combination looked tempting in the reel, and I was curious to try it myself.

## <u>The Cafe</u>

As soon as I entered the cafe, I realised how small it was.

The place could barely accommodate **10–12 people**. The seating arrangement was quite cramped, and if two people were sitting across from each other, walking through the small aisle became difficult.

Anyway, I found a table and placed my order.

## <u>Molten Cookie with Ice Cream</u>

I ordered the Molten Cookie with Ice Cream. The waiter informed me that it would take around **15 minutes** to prepare, and I agreed to wait.

First, the waiter brought a cup containing a scoop of vanilla ice cream. Then came the hot molten cookie, which he carefully placed over the cold ice cream.

The chocolate filling inside the cookie was already melted. As soon as the hot cookie was placed on the ice cream, the chocolate cream and the outer cookie layer started separating.

It looked interesting, but the actual experience was a completely different story.

I took my first bite of the cookie, and honestly, **I wasn't impressed at all**.

In fact, my immediate reaction was regret!

The cookie was dry, and the chocolate filling inside was extremely dense. You know how you can tell that chocolate is too dense? It sticks to your spoon, and you have to put in extra effort just to get it off!

I thought maybe having the cookie with vanilla ice cream would make the combination taste better.

**But no, that didn't help either.**

The vanilla ice cream had no soul in it. There wasn't any strong vanilla flavour, and the ice cream didn't even feel cold.

I understand that placing a hot molten cookie over cold ice cream would affect its temperature. But I still felt that the ice cream shouldn't have lost its coldness so quickly.

## <u>Was It Anything Like a Sizzling Brownie?</u>

The concept of this dessert is quite similar to a **sizzling brownie with ice cream** — a warm, chocolatey dessert served with cold ice cream.

However, I feel the ice cream made this experience awful.

The whole point of this combination is to enjoy the contrast between something warm and chocolatey and a scoop of cold, creamy ice cream. Unfortunately, the ice cream here didn't have a strong vanilla flavour and didn't even feel cold enough.

I believe a good-quality vanilla ice cream could have made a significant difference to the overall experience.

## <u>Value for Money</u>

The dessert cost **₹325**.

Honestly, I found it way too expensive, especially considering my experience with it.

For ₹325, I expected a much better combination of a soft, delicious cookie, rich chocolate filling and cold vanilla ice cream.

Unfortunately, this one didn't meet my expectations.

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

This was another instance where a social media reel convinced me to try something new, but the actual experience didn't match what I had imagined.

The molten cookie was dry, the chocolate filling was too dense, and the vanilla ice cream didn't add much to the experience.

**Would I spend ₹325 on this again? Probably not.**

Not every viral dessert turns out to be worth the hype, and this was one of those experiences!
