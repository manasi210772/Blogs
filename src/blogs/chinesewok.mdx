## <u>Introduction</u>

On **22nd August 2026**, I visited **Lakeshore Mall** with my mother. I was planning to have lunch there, but there was one small restriction—I was observing **Shravan**, so I was supposed to eat only vegetarian food.

Now, I have to admit that I'm **not exactly a fan of vegetarian meals**. But if I find something interesting, with **bold flavors** and something that isn't usually cooked at home, I am always willing to give it a try.

So naturally, my first thought was: **Domino's Pizza!**

I love pizzas that are loaded with vegetables, so I thought it would be a perfect vegetarian lunch.

Unfortunately, when we reached the Domino's outlet, we were told that it was **under maintenance** and would only resume operations after an hour or two.

There went my pizza plan.

My mother and I started walking around again, looking for some vegetarian fast food. That's when I spotted **Chinese Wok**.

I had been wanting to try Chinese Wok for quite some time, and thankfully, my mother agreed too. So we finally decided to give it a try.

After going through the menu, we ordered:

• Veg Spring Rolls  
• Super Bowl – Manchurian Gravy with Hakka Noodles  
• Super Bowl – Manchurian Gravy with Fried Rice  

And honestly, this unexpected change in plans turned out to be pretty good!

<br></br>

## <u>What We Ordered</u>

One thing I have to say right away—**vegetarian food is definitely cheaper!**

All three dishes together came to **under ₹500**, which I found quite reasonable.

Another thing I noticed was that **everything was served piping hot**. And I mean *really* hot!

Let's get into the dishes.

<br></br>

## <u>Veg Spring Rolls</u>

We started with the **Veg Spring Rolls**.

They were **crispy on the outside**, while the inside contained some **Hakka noodles and a few smashed vegetables**.

Interestingly, the vegetables inside tasted almost exactly like the mixture used in the Manchurian balls. So I'm guessing the same mixture might have been used for the spring rolls as well.

We were served **six finger-sized spring rolls**, which were just the right size for a quick bite.

What I really liked was that they weren't oily at all.

Crispy, hot, and not greasy—**a really good start to the meal.**

<br></br>

## <u>Super Bowl – Manchurian Gravy with Hakka Noodles</u>

This was **my favorite dish of the meal**.

The portion came in a big bowl. The **left half was filled with Hakka noodles**, while the **right half had Manchurian gravy with three Manchurian balls**.

By the time the bowl reached us, some of the gravy had already mixed into the noodles at the bottom.

And honestly, I loved that.

I personally find eating noodles on their own a little dry, so I really enjoy having them with gravy.

The food was **piping hot**—so hot that my mouth was practically getting burned after every bite!

The Manchurian balls themselves were also **very soft** and broke apart easily.

The only problem was the amount of gravy.

They were quite generous with it, and the gravy had a tendency to **splash everywhere** whenever I tried taking a sip or eating the noodles.

So, a small tip from my experience: **carry enough tissue paper!**

The **Hakka noodles** were quite subtle in flavor and, importantly, **not very oily**.

The Manchurian gravy, however, was a completely different story.

I could actually see a **layer of oil at the bottom of the bowl**, so if you're someone who is conscious about oily food, that's something to keep in mind.

The gravy also didn't have those extremely bold flavors that you might get from a street-side Chinese stall.

But despite that, **it was still tasty and I genuinely enjoyed it.**

<br></br>

## <u>Super Bowl – Manchurian Gravy with Fried Rice</u>

My mother ordered the **Super Bowl with Manchurian Gravy and Fried Rice**.

I didn't taste this one, so I can't really tell you how the fried rice tasted.

I can only assume that the **Manchurian gravy was similar to the one I had**, but I won't rate or review the fried rice since I didn't actually try it.

<br></br>

## <u>Overall Chinese Wok Experience</u>

Instead of rating each individual dish, I'm giving this visit an **overall rating**.

### ⭐ Overall Rating: 10/10

I absolutely **love Chinese food**, so perhaps it isn't surprising that I enjoyed this meal so much.

But beyond that, I genuinely liked my experience at Chinese Wok. The food was hot, the portions were filling, and the Hakka noodles with Manchurian gravy were exactly the kind of combination I enjoy.

What started as a failed attempt to get pizza ended up introducing me to a place I would happily try again.

**I would definitely give Chinese Wok another try!**

<br></br>

## <u>Ending the Meal with an Oreo Frappe</u>

On the same day, I also had an **Oreo Frappe from McDonald's**.

After walking around the mall and having lunch, it was a nice drink to cool down.

It contained **milk, whipped cream, and crushed Oreo biscuits**.

It was sweet, refreshing, and exactly what I wanted at that moment.

### ⭐ Rating: 8.5/10
