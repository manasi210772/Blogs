## <u>Introduction</u>

Sometimes, the best food stories begin with plans that don't go as expected.

One evening, my cousin and I decided that pizza would be the perfect dinner. After discussing the options for quite a while, we settled on **Oven Story Pizza**. We carefully selected a few pizzas and side dishes, added everything to the cart, and decided to place the order around **8:30 PM**.

However, when I opened Swiggy to finally place the order, every single pizza and side dish from Oven Story had suddenly become unavailable. The restaurant page was only showing beverages and juices.

With our original dinner plans cancelled at the last minute, we quickly switched to **PizzaExpress** instead.

<br></br>


## <u>The Delivery Experience</u>

After placing the order, Swiggy estimated a delivery time of around **40 minutes**.

By the time we ordered, it was already **9:00 PM**.

At **9:40 PM**, the app showed that the order would arrive in another **7 minutes**.

I checked again around **9:50 PM**, but surprisingly, it still displayed **7 minutes**.

Since everyone at home was getting hungry, I even messaged the delivery partner through the app asking how much longer it would take. I never received a reply, which was understandable since he was probably driving.

When he finally arrived after **10:00 PM**, he explained that **PizzaExpress usually hands over orders around 20 minutes later than expected**, making it appear on the app as though the delivery partner was taking extra time.

By then, everyone was so hungry that we immediately started eating.

Unfortunately, I couldn't click any pictures of the food because dinner had already become a little too late!

<br></br>

## <u>What We Ordered</u>
<br></br>

### <u><b>Garlic Bread Mozzarella</b></u>

This was a garlic bread topped with **garlic butter** and **melted mozzarella cheese**.

While the cheese itself was delicious, I expected a much stronger garlic flavour. Overall, it felt a little bland for my taste.

### ⭐ Rating: 6/10

<br></br>

### <u><b>Spicy Bruschetta</b></u>

This turned out to be one of the better starters.

It consisted of freshly baked dough topped with **mozzarella cheese** and **jalapeños**, finished with **rocket leaves** and **Emilgrana cheese**.

The jalapeños added a pleasant spicy kick that balanced the richness of the cheese.

Interestingly, this meal taught me two new things:
- **Rocket** refers to the leafy greens used as a topping.
- **Emilgrana** is the cheese sprinkled over the bruschetta.

Personally, I'm not a fan of eating raw leaves, so I left the rocket aside.

### ⭐ Rating: 7.5/10

<br></br>

### <u><b>Creamy Alfredo Veggie Pizza</b></u>

This pizza came topped with:

• Creamy Alfredo sauce                                                      
• Mushrooms                                                             
• Bell peppers                                                                 
• Onions                                                              
• Mozzarella cheese                                                                    
• Fresh basil                                                               
• Balsamic glaze                                                   
• Chilli oil                                                           

This was probably the most unique dish of the evening.

The **balsamic glaze** added a subtle sweetness that paired surprisingly well with the creamy Alfredo base.

The thin crust tasted fresh, and the combination of toppings worked beautifully together.

### ⭐ Rating: 9/10

This is definitely a pizza I'd recommend trying.

<br></br>

### <u><b>Chicken Overloaded Pizza</b></u>

The second pizza featured:

• Malai chicken                                                                            
• Tandoori chicken                                                                             
• Grilled chicken                                                                       
• Onion                                                                
• Red paprika                                                                        
• Mozzarella cheese                                                                  
• Passata                                                    

The chicken was juicy, tender, and generously distributed across the pizza.

The **passata** and **paprika** balanced the richness of the chicken nicely, making every bite enjoyable.

### ⭐ Rating: 9/10

<br></br>

## <u>A Small Observation</u>

Although both pizzas had a **thin crust**, the outer edges were surprisingly thick and quite dry.

Chewing through the crust actually required a bit of effort, and I personally wished it had been softer.

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

Even though our original dinner plans changed unexpectedly and the delivery took much longer than expected, the meal itself turned out to be enjoyable.

The **Creamy Alfredo Veggie Pizza** and **Chicken Overloaded Pizza** were definitely the highlights of the evening and are dishes I'd happily order again.

Hopefully, next time the pizzas arrive a little earlier... and I'll finally get the chance to take some pictures before everyone finishes eating!
