## <u>Introduction</u>

In our culture, we have one month known as **Shraavan**, during which we are not supposed to eat non-veg.

As someone who absolutely loves non-veg food, this is a very difficult month for me!

So, finally, **today our Shraavan month ended**, and we decided to celebrate by having lunch at a restaurant.

In Thane's **Panchpakhadi** area, there is a restaurant called **Hotel Malvan**, which serves authentic Maharashtrian cuisine. I have been to this restaurant many times, but today's visit was special because of one thing — **crab!**

<br></br>

## <u>What We Ordered</u>

Today we ordered:

• **Crab Masala**
• **Surmai Fry**
• **Ravas Fry**
• **Rice Bhakri & Rice**

## <u>Crab Masala</u>

I have always had **crab curry at home**, so I wanted to try it at a restaurant at least once and experience some new flavours.

The crab masala came with **two centre portions of the crab, along with two legs**. The curry had deep flavours and was slightly thick.

The size of the crabs was medium, and they were easy enough to crack with my teeth.

But after cracking into those legs — **oh my god!**

The crab was so juicy that my plate literally got filled with all the juices.

The crab itself and the crab curry were **mind-blowing**. I had the curry with both **rice bhakri and rice**, and it tasted amazing with both.

**⭐ Rating: 10/10**

<br></br>

## <u>Surmai Fry</u>

The **Surmai Fry** was very fresh, and the masala coating was really tasty.

It wasn't too spicy and wasn't too bland either — it was just perfect.

The piece of Surmai was also big enough for two people, so we shared it between the two of us.

**⭐ Rating: 9/10**

## <u>Ravas Fry</u>

The **Ravas Fry** felt more like a starter dish, but I had it along with the rice and crab curry.

It was served as **boneless fish cubes**, which made it very easy to eat.

The fish was fresh and the dish was very delicious.

**⭐ Rating: 9/10**

<br></br>

## <u>Rice Bhakri & Rice</u>

The **rice bhakri** was thin, soft and evenly cooked.

The rice was **long-grain rice**, although it wasn't basmati, and it was still good.

The crab curry tasted really good with both the bhakri and the rice. I personally enjoyed having the crab curry with both.

<br></br>

## <u>One More Thing You Can Try!</u>

If you visit Hotel Malvan, you can also try the **Bombil Fry** as a starter.

It is **deep-fried and coated with rava**, making it a good appetizer.

**Must try!**

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

After surviving an entire month without non-veg, this was exactly the kind of lunch I wanted!

The **Crab Masala was definitely the highlight** of the meal for me. The deep-flavoured curry combined with the juicy crab made it something I really enjoyed.

And after finally getting to eat seafood again, I can confidently say — **Shraavan was difficult, but this lunch made up for it!**
