## <u>Have You Ever Heard of Frullato?</u>

Have you guys ever heard of **Frullato**?

Frullato is an outlet that sells melted vanilla ice cream mixed with different chocolatey ingredients to create delicious desserts.

I tried three of their Frullatos:

- **Classic Oreo**
- **Brownie Rocks**
- **Chocolava**

And here's what I thought of each one!

## <u>Classic Oreo</u>

You get a medium-sized cup filled with melted vanilla ice cream mixed with powdered Oreo biscuits. At the top, they add an Oreo biscuit along with a layer of crushed Oreo biscuits beside it. There's another Oreo biscuit at the bottom of the cup, too!

The Oreo biscuits soak up the vanilla ice cream, and the combination tastes **too good**.

What makes this dessert interesting is its texture. It's cold like ice cream, but it isn't as solid. You get that creamy, semi-melted consistency that makes every spoonful enjoyable.

I loved how the Oreo biscuits blended with the vanilla ice cream. It's a simple combination, but it works really well!

## <u>Brownie Rocks</u>

The Brownie Rocks Frullato comes in a medium-sized cup, just like the Classic Oreo. The base is melted vanilla ice cream mixed with powdered Oreo biscuits, but this time, they add **chocolate chunks and brownie pieces on top**.

The chocolate chunks add a lovely chocolate flavour and a bit of crunch, while the brownie pieces soak up the vanilla ice cream. Together, they taste absolutely delicious!

What I particularly liked was how the subtle vanilla flavour balanced the otherwise overpowering chocolate flavour. The vanilla ice cream wraps around all that chocolatey goodness and brings the flavours together beautifully.

The melted vanilla ice cream also gives the dessert a feeling similar to having a **cold chocolate milkshake**.

And honestly, it reminded me of **Keventers**! I haven't tried Keventers in a while now, and I really miss it.

## <u>Chocolava</u>

My experience with Chocolava was a little different from the other two.

I couldn't have it on the same day I ordered it, so I stored it in the refrigerator and ate it almost **15 hours later**.

Because of this, I assume the Chocolava portion had become a little compact.

This Frullato had three layers:

- **Top layer:** Chocolate mousse
- **Middle layer:** Chocolate cake
- **Bottom layer:** Melted vanilla ice cream with a hint of chocolate flavour

The chocolate mousse on top had become quite compact, possibly because of the refrigeration. I would have preferred it to be a little more airy.

The chocolate cake layer was good, and the melted vanilla ice cream underneath had a subtle chocolate flavour.

Interestingly, this Frullato didn't contain Oreo biscuit crumbs, unlike the other two I tried.

One thing I noticed was that even after being stored in the refrigerator, the ice cream hadn't frozen solid. So, the texture of the Frullato was maintained.

However, I can't say whether the compact mousse was how it was originally meant to be, since I ate it much later. Perhaps the experience would have been different if I'd had it on the same day!
