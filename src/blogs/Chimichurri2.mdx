## <u>Introduction</u>

On **9th August 2026**, I found myself at **Chimichurri – The Asian Kitchen** once again.

After my first visit, where I tried dimsum and ramen for the very first time, I was excited to come back and explore some more dishes from their menu.

Since it was a **Sunday**, we already knew that the restaurant would be crowded. This time, we decided to reserve a table beforehand. However, even with a reservation, we still had to wait for around **15–20 minutes** before getting our table.

Once we were seated, we ordered quite a few dishes:

• Crab Stick and Egg Drop Soup                                                                            
• Crispy Tempura Shrimp                                                                               
• Tai Pai Chicken                                                                                                
• Chicken Satay with Peanut Butter Sauce                                                                                   
• Hot Paper Pot – Chicken Thai Green Curry with Jasmine Rice                                                                            
• Chimichurri Fried Rice  

This visit turned out to be quite different from my first one, with some dishes that surprised me and others that didn't quite live up to my expectations.

## <u>Crab Stick and Egg Drop Soup</u>

The first dish to arrive was the **Crab Stick and Egg Drop Soup**.

The soup was quite **watery**, but it was still very flavorful and subtle in taste. Every sip had a distinct crab-like flavor, which made the soup quite enjoyable.

However, one of the people accompanying me mentioned that it might not actually be crab, but rather a type of fish that tastes like crab. I'm not completely sure about that, so I'll leave that part to my fellow food explorers!

Regardless of what was actually in it, I found the soup to be **refreshing and warm**. It was a lovely way to start the meal.

### ⭐ Rating: 9.5/10

## <u>Crispy Tempura Shrimp</u>

Next came the **Crispy Tempura Shrimp**.

We had asked for **four pieces**.

This was actually sushi, and I'm still not completely sure whether the shrimp itself was cooked or not. I decided to play it safe and only had **half a piece** to see how it tasted.

Unfortunately, the only flavor I could really taste was **cheese**, because there was cheese on top of the sushi.

The texture was also very much like eating rice, which wasn't particularly exciting for me.

Because I didn't really eat enough of the dish to properly judge it, **I won't be giving this one a rating**.

<br></br>

## <u>Tai Pai Chicken</u>

The **Tai Pai Chicken** was probably one of my favorite dishes from this visit.

The moment I tasted it, it literally **activated my taste buds with its spice**!

It tasted exactly like the kind of **Indo-Chinese dish** that I'm familiar with. Later, I actually googled Tai Pai Chicken and found out that it is indeed an Indo-Chinese dish.

The dish mainly consisted of **chicken and onions**, along with the usual Chinese-style sauces.

The quantity was also quite sufficient, making it a dish that everyone could enjoy.

### ⭐ Rating: 9.5/10

<br></br>

## <u>Chicken Satay with Peanut Butter Sauce</u>

I had **high hopes for the chicken satay**, but unfortunately, it didn't quite meet my expectations.

The chicken was roasted and was actually **tender and juicy**, but the spices drizzled over it tasted a little strange to me.

For some reason, the flavor reminded me of **dal khichdi**!

The chicken was served on skewers, and personally, I wish it had been a little **spicier**.

However, the saving grace of this dish was definitely the **peanut sauce**.

It genuinely tasted like peanut and complemented the chicken really well. It actually helped uplift the dish for me.

### ⭐ Rating: 7.5/10

<br></br>

## <u>Hot Paper Pot – Chicken Thai Green Curry</u>

Now this was something **completely new to me**.

We ordered the **Hot Paper Pot – Chicken Thai Green Curry with Jasmine Rice**, and the presentation itself caught my attention.

There was a **small candle lit underneath a container**, while a thick paper-like layer was held on a support above it. The Thai green curry was placed on top.

I believe the idea was to **keep the curry warm throughout the meal**. Although, after some time, I wasn't entirely sure whether it was still keeping the curry warm!

But the curry itself? I really liked it.

The **Thai green curry was subtle and pleasant**, without any particularly strong flavors overpowering the dish.

It contained: Chicken, Mushroom, Broccoli, Cabbage leaves, and other ingredients  

The **jasmine rice** that came with it was fresh, slightly sticky, and fragrant. It paired really well with the curry.

### ⭐ Rating: 9/10

## <u>Chimichurri Fried Rice</u>

The last dish we tried was the **Chimichurri Fried Rice**.

At first bite, it tasted very similar to the **Indo-Chinese fried rice** that I'm used to having.

The difference was that this one had **subtle flavors and noticeably less oil**.

It contained several vegetables along with pieces of **egg omelette**, which added some nice texture to the rice.

Overall, it was simple, flavorful, and enjoyable.

I also tried mixing some of the **Thai green curry with the fried rice**, and surprisingly, that combination tasted pretty good too!

### ⭐ Rating: 8.5/10

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

My second visit to **Chimichurri – The Asian Kitchen** gave me a completely different experience from my first one.

The **Crab Stick and Egg Drop Soup** was warm, refreshing, and full of flavor, while the **Tai Pai Chicken** stood out with its spicy Indo-Chinese flavors.

The **Thai green curry** was another favorite of mine, especially because of how subtle and balanced it tasted. The jasmine rice paired beautifully with it as well.

The **chicken satay** didn't quite live up to my expectations, although the peanut sauce definitely helped. The crispy tempura shrimp was also not something I could properly judge because I only tried half a piece.

Overall, there were definitely some hits and misses, but I enjoyed exploring more of Chimichurri's menu.

And yes, **I think there will probably be a third visit!**
