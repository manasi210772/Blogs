## <u>Introduction</u>

After getting influenced by social media reels, I decided to try **Mahabi Cafe Patisserie Gelato** at Manpada, Thane.

The shop is located in a very isolated place, where there isn't much development in the surrounding area.

Before entering the shop, I noticed a few chairs and tables outside, arranged in a very small space.

When I entered, I was greeted by a huge variety of desserts. However, the shop itself was very small. I don't think more than **4–5 customers** could comfortably fit inside at once. So, I was actually glad that I had decided to take the desserts home and enjoy them there. 😄

<br></br>

## 🍰 <u>The Desserts That Caught My Attention</u>

The reel I had seen featured the **viral London Slice**, and it looked quite tempting. So, that was something I was definitely going to buy!

Apart from that, there were many other sweet dishes from around the world, including **Tiramisu, Macarons, Donuts**, and more.

I had always seen macarons being featured in **MasterChef** and had wanted to try them for a long time. So, when I saw that I could pick individual macarons instead of buying them as a set, I decided to give them a try.

I have generally observed that at **Theobroma**, macarons are not available most of the time, and when they are available, you usually need to buy them as a set. I personally don't like that, so being able to pick individual macarons here was something I liked.

<br></br>

## 🛍️ <u>What I Bought</u>

I bought three items:

- **Viral London Slice**
- **Chocolate Macaron**
- **Nutella Macaron**

## 💰 <u>Value for Money</u>

This place is **very expensive**.

The **150g London Slice cost ₹360**, while the macarons were **₹100 each**.

But since I am a foodie and wanted to explore this place, I decided to ignore the cost and enjoy the experience. 😄

<br></br>

## ☕ <u>One Suggestion</u>

The salesperson suggested that **Tiramisu is their speciality**.

However, I had already tried Tiramisu before, although not at Mahabi, and I was also being a bit calorie conscious that day. So, I decided not to buy it.

But if you visit Mahabi, you can definitely give their Tiramisu a try!

<br></br>

## 🍫 <u>Viral London Slice</u>

This was **my favourite** out of everything I bought!

The London Slice was **very creamy and very chocolaty**. The outer chocolate layer was a little bitter, but the inner layers were delicious.

The inside had layers of **soft chocolate cake sponge and chocolate cream**. The sponge was light and airy, which I really loved.

What made the texture even better was that the sponge had soaked up the moisture from the cream. This made the overall texture of the London Slice even more enjoyable.

**Rating: 9/10 ⭐**

<br></br>

## 🍓 <u>Chocolate Macaron</u>

The **red-coloured macaron** was the chocolate macaron.

The biscuit part seemed to have either a **strawberry or raspberry** flavour. It had a hint of some fruity essence to it.

However, I found the biscuit to be quite dry. When you take the first bite, the shell only needs a soft bite to crack open, but the top of the macaron felt a little powdery.

Another thing I noticed was that the **chocolate filling between the two macaron shells was very little compared to the biscuit**.

Because of this, the fruity flavour was overpowering the chocolate flavour. I feel that if there had been a little more chocolate filling, the flavours would have been better balanced.

<br></br>

## 🍫 <u>Nutella Macaron</u>

The Nutella macaron was quite similar to the chocolate macaron.

The main difference was that it did not have the strawberry or raspberry essence.

I couldn't really figure out what essence was used in the biscuit of this macaron. I also couldn't clearly differentiate between the **chocolate flavour and the Nutella flavour**. They tasted quite similar to me.

<br></br>

## 🤔 <u>Should I Even Rate the Macarons?</u>

This is where I am a little confused. 😅

I have **never really tried a macaron before**, so I don't know what a perfectly made macaron is supposed to taste or feel like.

Because of that, I don't feel confident giving these macarons a rating. I can tell you what I personally liked and didn't like about them, but I don't think it would be fair for me to judge them against a standard when I haven't experienced one before.

So, for now, **no rating for the macarons!**

<br></br>

## ❄️ <u>Storage & Shelf Life</u>

All the products are supposed to be **consumed within 24 hours** and require **refrigeration**.

<br></br>

## <u>Final Thoughts</u>

My main reason for visiting Mahabi was the **viral London Slice**, and it ended up being my favourite item from everything I bought.

The macarons were also something I had been wanting to try for a long time, especially after seeing them so often on MasterChef. While I wasn't completely sure how to judge them, I was still happy that I finally got to try them individually.

Overall, this was another one of those visits where **social media reels successfully convinced me to try something new!** 😄
